Why We Gather

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada carried the chanting of the Hare Krishna mahā-mantra from India to the world, founding ISKCON and asking his students to carry it further still. The Legendary Festival is our way of saying thank you — a full day to gather as one family, through kirtan, class, and prasādam, and to celebrate the life and mission of the man who gave so many of us a spiritual home.

“Chant Hare Krishna and be happy!”

— Srila Prabhupada

Every kirtan, every plate of prasādam, every conversation between old friends and new ones — it's all part of that same sankirtan spirit. Whether you've been a devotee for decades or you're curious and stepping in for the very first time, there's a place set for you here.
